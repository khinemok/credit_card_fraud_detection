# Research Questions

Central question: To what extent do data leakage, evaluation strategy, and class-imbalance handling affect the reliability of measured machine-learning performance in credit-card fraud detection?

RQ1. Under leakage-free chronological evaluation, how do Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, and Random Forest compare in average precision and fraud-detection performance?

RQ2. How do original training data, class weighting, and SMOTE affect performance, and do effects differ by model?

RQ3. How do deliberately flawed SMOTE-before-split evaluation, clean random splitting, and clean chronological splitting affect measured performance and model rankings?

RQ4. How do validation-selected thresholds and assumed review costs affect the trade-off between fraud detection, false alarms, and estimated operational cost?
